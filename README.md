# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 13, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Locker Buddy** ("the App"), developed by **Paul Savu** ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the App.

By using Locker Buddy, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

Locker Buddy is designed to provide its core functionality while minimizing the amount of personal information collected from users.

Depending on how you use the App and the services integrated into it, the following information may be processed:

### Information stored by the App

Information that you create or enter while using Locker Buddy, such as locker-related information, may be stored locally on your device to provide the App's functionality.

We do not sell this information.

### Advertising information

Locker Buddy uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements.

AdMob and its associated services may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location, app usage information, and information about advertisements displayed or interacted with, depending on your device settings, consent choices, and applicable Google policies.

This information may be used to provide, personalize, measure, and improve advertising.

For more information about Google's handling of data, please refer to Google's privacy information:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

### In-app purchases

Locker Buddy offers optional in-app purchases through **Google Play Billing**.

When you make a purchase, the transaction is processed by Google Play. Locker Buddy does not receive or store your payment card number, bank account information, or other payment credentials.

Google may process information associated with the purchase in accordance with Google's own privacy policies and terms.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information processed by Locker Buddy and its integrated services may be used to:

* Provide and maintain the App;
* Store and manage locker information created by you;
* Provide optional in-app purchases;
* Display advertisements;
* Measure and improve advertising performance;
* Maintain the security and functionality of the App;
* Diagnose technical problems and improve the user experience.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 3. Third-Party Services

Locker Buddy uses third-party services that may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

These services may include:

* **Google AdMob** — for displaying and measuring advertisements.
* **Google Play Billing** — for processing in-app purchases.

These third-party providers may collect information directly from your device or receive information necessary to provide their services.

Their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

## 4. Advertising

Locker Buddy may display advertisements through Google AdMob.

Advertising may involve the processing of device and advertising-related information, including advertising identifiers and information about interactions with advertisements.

Where required by applicable law or Google policies, applicable consent mechanisms may be presented to users regarding personalized advertising.

You may also be able to control advertising-related settings through your Android device or Google account settings.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Information stored locally by Locker Buddy remains on your device unless otherwise stated in this Privacy Policy.

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-party services used by the App are responsible for implementing their own security measures for information they process.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Locker Buddy does not maintain a personal user account database for the App's core functionality.

Information stored locally by the App generally remains on your device until you delete it through the App, clear the App's data, uninstall the App, or otherwise remove the relevant information.

Information processed by third-party services, such as Google AdMob and Google Play, is retained and handled according to their respective policies.

If you have questions regarding information associated with your use of Locker Buddy, you may contact us using the contact information provided below.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Locker Buddy is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

## 8. Your Choices

You may:

* Stop using Locker Buddy at any time;
* Delete locally stored App data through your Android device settings;
* Uninstall the App;
* Manage advertising preferences through available Android or Google settings;
* Choose whether to make optional in-app purchases.

The availability of particular advertising controls may depend on your device, location, and applicable regulations.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Locker Buddy, the services used by the App, or applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy will be updated.
